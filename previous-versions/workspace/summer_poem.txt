The sun spills gold, a liquid light,
Across the meadows, pure and bright.
The whisper of the summer breeze,
Rustling through the emerald trees.

Barefoot on grass, the days unfold,
Stories whispered, new and old.
Cicadas hum a drowsy tune,
Beneath the brilliant summer noon.

Crimson sunsets paint the sky,
As golden moments drift on by.
Sweet scent of jasmine, warm and deep,
Secrets that the summer loves to keep.
